Company profile

Precision at every stage

Based in Southerton, Harare, SMARTMEC combines skilled workmanship with modern diagnostic and repair equipment. We restore vehicle performance and appearance with a focus on quality, transparency and customer satisfaction.

Our team serves individuals, fleet owners, motor institutions and insurance clients — delivering clear communication from first inspection through to handover.

  • Mechanical repairs, servicing and diagnostics
  • Panel beating, chassis alignment and spray painting
  • Hybrid diagnostics and auto electrical work
  • Insurance-supported accident repairs
  • 24-hour towing assistance
